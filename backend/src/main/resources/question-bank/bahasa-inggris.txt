== Bahasa Inggris | 5 | What are you doing?

‘They ... playing football now.’ The correct word is ... | are | is | am | does | With ‘they’, we use ‘are’.
‘Look! The baby ... sleeping.’ The correct word is ... | is | are | am | do | With one person or thing, we use ‘is’.
The -ing form of ‘swim’ is ... | swimming | swiming | swimmeing | swims | Double the last consonant: swim → swimming.
The -ing form of ‘dance’ is ... | dancing | danceing | dancring | dances | Drop the final -e: dance → dancing.
